You have two ages. Your **chronological age** is how many years you have been alive. Your **biological age** is how old your body actually is — based on the state of your cells, organs, metabolism, and immune system.

These two numbers can diverge dramatically.<Cite id="belsky-2015-biological-aging-young-adults" /> A 50-year-old marathon runner who sleeps well, eats clean, and has excellent blood work might have the biology of a 38-year-old. A sedentary, stressed, metabolically unhealthy 35-year-old might have the biology of someone a decade older.

Biological age is not a fixed number. It is a trajectory — and it is modifiable.<Cite id="waziry-2023-calerie-caloric-restriction" /> That is what makes it the single most important metric in [longevity medicine](/blog/biomarkers-to-track-for-longevity).

## Why biological age matters

Chronological age alone is an imperfect predictor of health outcomes. Two 55-year-olds can have radically different risks of heart disease, cancer, cognitive decline, and death.<Cite id="liu-2018-phenotypic-age-nhanes" /> Biological age captures what chronological age cannot: the **cumulative effect of your genetics, environment, behaviors, and exposures** on your actual physiology.<Cite id="liu-2019-phenoage-health-retirement-study" />

Research shows that biological age predicts these outcomes even after accounting for chronological age:

- **All-cause mortality** — how likely you are to die from any cause in a given period<Cite id="liu-2018-phenotypic-age-nhanes" />
- **Cardiovascular disease risk**<Cite id="lu-2019-grimage" />
- **Cancer incidence**<Cite id="lu-2019-grimage" />
- **[Cognitive decline and dementia](/blog/brain-aging-biomarkers-cognitive-decline)**<Cite id="belsky-2015-biological-aging-young-adults" /><Cite id="levine-2018-phenoage" />
- **Physical frailty and disability**<Cite id="levine-2018-phenoage" />

If you could only track one meta-metric for your health, biological age would be it.

## How biological age is measured

### 1. PhenoAge (biomarker-based)

Developed by **Morgan Levine and colleagues at UCLA** and published in *Aging* in 2018, PhenoAge uses **9 routine blood biomarkers** plus chronological age to [estimate biological age](/blog/biological-age-calculator-blood-test):<Cite id="levine-2018-phenoage" />

- Albumin
- Creatinine
- Glucose (fasting)
- C-reactive protein (CRP)
- Lymphocyte percentage
- Mean cell volume (MCV)
- Red cell distribution width (RDW)
- Alkaline phosphatase
- White blood cell count

These markers span liver function, kidney function, metabolic health, inflammation, and immune status. PhenoAge was built on mortality data from NHANES III (a large US population study) and validated in an independent sample from the later NHANES IV survey,<Cite id="levine-2018-phenoage" /><Cite id="liu-2018-phenotypic-age-nhanes" /> and it has since been studied in other cohorts, including the US Health and Retirement Study.<Cite id="liu-2019-phenoage-health-retirement-study" />

**Advantages:** Uses standard blood tests that any lab can run. Cheap. Repeatable. Actionable — you can track changes over time.

**Limitations:** Does not capture epigenetic changes. Less granular than DNA methylation clocks.

### 2. Epigenetic clocks (DNA methylation)

Epigenetic clocks analyze **DNA methylation patterns** at specific sites across your genome. As you age, certain CpG sites gain or lose methyl groups in predictable patterns. By measuring hundreds to thousands of these sites, algorithms can estimate a person's age with remarkable accuracy.<Cite id="horvath-raj-2018-epigenetic-clock-theory" />

Major epigenetic clocks:

- **Horvath clock** (2013) — the original multi-tissue clock, built on 353 CpG sites<Cite id="horvath-2013-dna-methylation-age" />
- **Hannum clock** (2013) — blood-based, 71 CpG sites<Cite id="hannum-2013-methylome-aging-rates" />
- **GrimAge** (2019) — incorporates DNA methylation proxies for smoking history and plasma proteins; one of the strongest epigenetic predictors of mortality<Cite id="lu-2019-grimage" /><Cite id="li-2020-nine-biological-ages" />
- **DunedinPACE** (2022) — measures the *pace* of aging (how fast you are aging right now) rather than cumulative biological age<Cite id="belsky-2022-dunedinpace" />

**Advantages:** Widely used in aging research.<Cite id="higgins-chen-2022-epigenetic-clock-reliability" /> Captures deep biological aging signals.

**Limitations:** Requires a specialized DNA methylation assay. Reliability varies: on six widely used clocks, repeat measurements of the same sample differed by up to 9 years, although newer versions built to fix this agree far more closely.<Cite id="higgins-chen-2022-epigenetic-clock-reliability" /> Not yet routine clinical practice.<Cite id="moqri-2023-biomarkers-of-aging" />

Companies like **TruDiagnostic** (TruAge) and **Elysium Health** (Index) offer consumer epigenetic age testing.

### 3. Telomere length

Telomeres are protective caps on the ends of your chromosomes that shorten with each cell division. Shorter telomeres are associated with aging, disease, and mortality.<Cite id="blackburn-2015-telomere-biology" />

**Advantages:** Intuitive concept. Available through consumer testing.

**Limitations:** High variability between tests. A single measurement is unreliable.<Cite id="martin-ruiz-2015-telomere-reproducibility" /> Telomere length does not change fast enough to track short-term interventions.<Cite id="steenstrup-2013-telomere-lengthening" /> Less predictive of mortality than epigenetic clocks or blood-biomarker scores.<Cite id="li-2020-nine-biological-ages" />

Telomere testing is interesting but not recommended as your primary aging metric.

## What accelerates biological aging

Research has linked several factors to accelerated aging:

- **Chronic inflammation** — [elevated CRP](/blog/understanding-crp-inflammation), IL-6, TNF-alpha. Inflammation is the through-line in nearly every age-related disease.<Cite id="franceschi-2018-inflammaging" />
- **Insulin resistance** — markers of metabolic syndrome are linked to faster epigenetic aging.<Cite id="quach-2017-epigenetic-clock-lifestyle" />
- **Poor sleep** — insomnia symptoms are linked to faster epigenetic aging and to signs of immune-cell aging.<Cite id="carroll-2017-insomnia-epigenetic-aging" />
- **Chronic psychological stress** — linked to shorter telomeres and faster epigenetic aging, possibly through cortisol signaling.<Cite id="epel-2004-stress-telomere-shortening" /><Cite id="zannas-2015-lifetime-stress-epigenetic-aging" />
- **Sedentary lifestyle** — lower physical activity is linked to faster epigenetic aging.<Cite id="fox-2023-physical-activity-epigenetic-ageing" />
- **Smoking** — linked to older epigenetic age, most strongly on the GrimAge clock, which includes a DNA methylation signature of smoking.<Cite id="lu-2019-grimage" /><Cite id="cardenas-2022-smoking-epigenetic-age" />
- **Excess alcohol** — alcohol dependence has been linked to faster epigenetic aging in blood and liver.<Cite id="rosen-2018-alcohol-dependence-epigenetic-age" />
- **Ultra-processed diet** — associated with accelerated biological aging in a large Italian cohort.<Cite id="esposito-2024-ultraprocessed-food-biological-aging" />
- **Environmental toxins** — air pollution in particular is consistently associated with older DNA methylation age.<Cite id="dhingra-2018-dna-methylation-age-environment" />

## What slows (or reverses) biological aging

The encouraging reality: biological age is plastic. Some interventions work, although the effects measured in trials so far are modest.<Cite id="waziry-2023-calerie-caloric-restriction" />

### Exercise

One of the best-supported habits for healthy aging. Higher physical activity is associated with slower epigenetic aging.<Cite id="fox-2023-physical-activity-epigenetic-ageing" /> US guidelines recommend 150-300 minutes per week of moderate-intensity activity plus muscle-strengthening work on 2 or more days.<Cite id="piercy-2018-physical-activity-guidelines" />

### Diet quality

Mediterranean and whole-food diets have been associated with slower epigenetic aging in several studies.<Cite id="quach-2017-epigenetic-clock-lifestyle" /><Cite id="gensous-2020-mediterranean-diet-epigenetic" /> The common thread is high intake of polyphenols, fiber, omega-3 fatty acids, and micronutrients alongside low intake of refined sugar and ultra-processed food.

### Sleep

7-9 hours of quality sleep per night.<Cite id="hirshkowitz-2015-sleep-duration-recommendations" /> In a large study of older women, insomnia symptoms were linked to accelerated epigenetic aging, while self-reported short sleep was not.<Cite id="carroll-2017-insomnia-epigenetic-aging" />

### Stress management

Chronic stress is linked to faster epigenetic aging.<Cite id="zannas-2015-lifetime-stress-epigenetic-aging" /> Meditation, breathwork, time in nature, and strong social connections are common ways to manage it, and long-term meditation practice has been linked to slower epigenetic aging in a small study.<Cite id="chaix-2017-meditation-epigenetic-clock" />

### Specific interventions with clinical evidence

- **Caloric restriction / time-restricted eating** — in the CALERIE trial, two years of calorie restriction modestly slowed the pace of biological aging measured by DunedinPACE, but did not significantly change PhenoAge or GrimAge.<Cite id="waziry-2023-calerie-caloric-restriction" />
- **Metformin** — the proposed TAME trial is designed to test metformin as an anti-aging drug.<Cite id="barzilai-2016-metformin-tame" /> Observational data are promising.<Cite id="campbell-2017-metformin-mortality" />
- **Rapamycin** — mTOR inhibition with rapamycin extends lifespan in diverse model organisms, and human trials have begun.<Cite id="mannick-2023-mtor-inhibitors" />
- **NAD+ precursors** (NMN, NR) — mixed clinical evidence but strong mechanistic rationale.<Cite id="freeberg-2023-nad-boosting-compounds" />
- **The TRIIM trial** — in this small trial of 10 men, a combination of growth hormone, DHEA, and metformin left epigenetic age about 1.5 years below baseline after 12 months (about 2.5 years younger than expected without treatment).<Cite id="fahy-2019-triim-epigenetic-reversal" />
